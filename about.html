Meet our CEO

Building a platform for opportunity.

“Your Vision, Our Expertise is more than a slogan. It is how we serve every client and every student.”

Ahmad Ismail Waziri is an entrepreneur, educator and technology-driven business leader passionate about building sustainable businesses and creating opportunities for young people.

As Founder and CEO of DanSebo Global Services Ltd, he leads the company with a clear vision to build a trusted brand across Printing, ICT, Education and Mentorship Services.

His approach combines creativity, technology, education and entrepreneurship to deliver practical solutions while empowering individuals and businesses to grow.

Driven by the belief in Your Vision, Our Expertise, he continues to build DanSebo as more than a business: a platform for opportunity, innovation and lasting impact.
